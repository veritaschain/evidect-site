URGENT SECURITY INFORMATION · FIXES AVAILABLE

Two critical vulnerabilities in PHP

Information checked: 21 October 2026

The PHP project has published fixes for two critical vulnerabilities that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution. If PHP handles web requests in your environment, contact your server administrator, maintenance partner or hosting provider and act promptly.

Your operating system or hosting provider may supply packages with backported fixes. A displayed version number alone does not establish patch status; check the provider's advisory.

For PHP 8.1 and earlier, check your vendor's fix availability or migrate to a supported branch.

What to do now

  1. Identify PHP use across public websites, ecommerce and member services, including the running versions and configurations.
  2. Apply a fixed release or the provider's patched package, then perform the required restarts and verify the running system.
  3. If you cannot update promptly, discuss restricting exposure or temporarily taking the affected service offline. Do not treat temporary measures as a complete fix.

Updating WordPress or its plugins is separate from addressing the PHP runtime on your server.

This is a general security advisory. It does not mean EVIDECT has confirmed a vulnerability, compromise or missing patch at any particular organization. Check the official sources below for updates before taking action.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does a notice mean my systems have been compromised?

No. This PHP notice shares an official critical security advisory and asks you to check your environment and apply the relevant fixes. It does not assert a confirmed vulnerability or compromise at your organization.

Is updating WordPress enough?

The issue affects PHP itself. Updating WordPress or its plugins does not necessarily patch the PHP runtime on the server. Check with the administrator or hosting provider responsible for PHP.

Do I need to reply or buy a service?

No purchase is required, and a reply to a general advisory is not mandatory. Prioritize reviewing the issue with your administrator or maintenance provider.

Can this contact provide emergency incident response?

This address handles questions about our notices. It does not guarantee round-the-clock monitoring or emergency incident response. If you suspect an incident, also contact your existing maintenance or incident response provider.
